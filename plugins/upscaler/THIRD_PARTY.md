## CNN shader sources

The optional neural upscale mode ships real-time CNN shader sources by
bloc97 (2019-2021) in the plugin's `assets/` directory. The restore, upscale and
highlight-clamp sources are licensed under the MIT License; the automatic
downscale helpers are released under the Unlicense. The full license text is kept at the top of every shader
source and is written together with it into the plugin data directory when the
mode is enabled.
